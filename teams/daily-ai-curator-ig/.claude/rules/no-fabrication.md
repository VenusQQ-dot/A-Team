# No Fabrication

## Applicability

- Applies to: All agents. Enforced at gates by `chief-editor` and at review by `fact-checker`.

## Rule Content

### Core Mandates

1. **CRITICAL: Never invent news.** Every factual claim in any deliverable must trace to a source ID in the research brief, and every source ID must resolve to a real, dated, reachable URL.
2. **No source, no definitive statement.** A claim without a source may not appear at all — not softened with 「據傳」、「聽說」, not framed as a question that implies the fact. Delete it or find the source.
3. **Numbers are the highest-risk claims.** Percentages, user counts, speed multipliers, and prices must match the source's exact figure and scope. "Significantly faster" in a source never becomes 「快 3 倍」 in copy.
4. **Scope words must survive translation.** Limited beta ≠ 全面開放; research preview ≠ 正式上線; US-only ≠ 開放使用. The fact-checker verifies scope words explicitly.
5. **Hard news must be translated, not distorted.** When converting news into workplace impact, the impact framing must be honestly derivable from the sourced fact. 「這對上班族的影響」 is interpretation and must be labeled as such when speculative (「可能」、「預期」 with the reasoning shown).

### Working Notation

Producers attach `[S#]` markers to every factual claim in drafts. The fact-checker verifies each, then strips markers from final copy. Unmarked factual statements are treated as unsourced and flagged `UNVERIFIED`.

### Recency Honesty

A story older than the 72h window may not be presented as 「今日」/「最新」 news. Evergreen technique content is allowed but must be framed as technique, not news.

## Violation Determination

- Any deliverable claim with no resolvable source ID → Violation
- A number or scope word differing from its source → Violation
- An unsourced claim retained in softened form (「據傳」) instead of deleted → Violation
- Old news framed as breaking → Violation

Violation scenario: caption adds 「已有 5 萬家企業導入」 to strengthen the hook; no brief source mentions any adoption figure. Softening it to 「據說很多企業都在用」 is equally a violation — the line must be deleted or re-anchored to a real source → Violation.

## Exceptions

- Clearly-labeled opinion and interpretation (「我們的看法」、「預期影響」) is allowed when the underlying facts are sourced and the inference is shown.

Tradeoff: this rule kills some of the punchiest hook lines and occasionally forces a weaker P1. Accept the weaker hook — one fabricated figure costs the account more trust than a hundred strong hooks earn.
