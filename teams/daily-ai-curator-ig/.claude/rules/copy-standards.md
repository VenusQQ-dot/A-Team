# Copy Standards

## Applicability

- Applies to: `topic-strategist`, `carousel-writer`, `caption-writer` (produce), `content-reviewer` (enforces)

## Rule Content

### Language

All reader-facing copy is Traditional Chinese. Tool names and technical terms stay in English (Claude, Higgsfield, Web Search, Prompt). Simplified Chinese characters are defects.

### Length Limits

- 主文案: ≤45 字 per page
- 補充說明: ≤80 字 per page
- Counting: CJK and Latin characters count 1 each; punctuation counts; spaces do not. Count, do not estimate.

### Tone — 短、狠、清楚

- Short declarative or imperative sentences; one idea per line
- 專業但不生硬: expert confidence without bureaucratic stiffness; light conversational bridges allowed in captions (「先說結論」), not in page copy
- Every 重點 page answers 「所以我明天上班可以怎麼用?」— capability descriptions without a reader action are incomplete
- No exclamation-mark stacking, no emoji in 主文案 (captions may use sparingly, ≤4 total)

### Positioning Filter

This account is 「每天一則 AI 職場應用輪播」, not an AI news digest. Copy framed as "X 公司發布了 Y" fails the filter; reframe as 「Y 能幫你做什麼」 with the news as evidence.

### Forbidden Patterns

Hedging and filler are prohibited in copy and internal recommendations: 「可能有幫助」、「值得關注」、「各有優缺點」、「大家可以試試看」、「等等」、「之類的」. Replace with a concrete claim, a concrete action, or delete the line. Vague quantifiers (「很多」、「大幅」) require a sourced number or get cut.

## Violation Determination

- Any 主文案 over 45 字 or 補充說明 over 80 字 (by count) → Violation
- Simplified characters in any deliverable → Violation
- A 重點 page (P3–P5) with no reader action → Violation
- Any forbidden hedging/filler pattern in reader-facing copy → Violation

Violation scenario: P4 主文案 reads 「這個新功能很強大,大家可以關注一下,說不定用得到」— hedged, no action, no substance. Correct shape: 「開會前 10 分鐘,用這句 prompt 讓 AI 幫你抓重點」 → Violation.

## Exceptions

- Direct quotations from sources keep their original wording and language, marked as quotes.

Tradeoff: the 45-character limit regularly forces cutting a genuinely useful second clause. Keep the strongest clause and move the rest to 補充說明 — density is the product.
